# Company Fit

Generated by `job-search-setup`. Input: a company name, sometimes a job link. Output: a one-screen brief in chat on what the company does, where it's going, and how well it fits what {{USER}} wants.

## Step 1: Load the context

Read these in parallel:
- `{{DATA}}/Profile.md`: experience, strengths, gaps, what {{USER}} wants, ranked priorities, trajectory, deal-breakers. This is the yardstick for every judgement. Where it says TODO, don't assume.
- `{{DATA}}/README.md`: is the company on the target list, and in which segment?
- `{{DATA}}/Job-Watch.md`: open roles there that `{{WATCH}}` has already found.
- `{{DATA}}/Companies/<Company>.md`, if a brief was saved before: start from it and say what changed.
- The tracker: any past application and its status (a recent rejection matters). {{TRACKER_READ}}

If the name is ambiguous, ask which company before researching.

## Step 2: Research the company

Use WebSearch (several searches in one turn) and WebFetch on the company's own site. Find:
- **What they do:** product, who buys it, business model, main competitors.
- **Size and stage:** founding year, HQ, headcount, funding (last round, amount, date, investors) or public status, revenue if known. Check the current status rather than trusting the target list's segment or an old brief: companies list, get acquired or raise new rounds, and that changes how they score against the profile's stage preference.
- **Trajectory:** where the company is heading. News from the last ~12 months: growth, launches, big customers, new markets, layoffs, leadership changes, regulatory trouble, acquisitions. Hiring trend: growing, flat, or shrinking teams.
- **What they're looking for:** from the job link if given, otherwise from their open roles. The skills, experience and traits they ask for again and again, and how the team {{USER}} would join is set up.
- **Presence where {{USER}} wants to work:** office in their location, remote policy, size of the local team.
- **Culture signals:** stated values (careers/culture page), Glassdoor or similar rating and recurring themes, office policy, pace, leadership reputation.
- **Pay:** stated ranges in postings, or typical ranges from public salary data; say which.
- **Open relevant roles:** check the careers page or job board (`.claude/skills/{{WATCH}}/companies.json` has the board for target companies) for roles matching {{USER}}'s titles and locations.

Prefer recent, primary sources. Date anything time-sensitive, and don't present an estimate as fact.

## Step 3: Judge the fit

Score the company against each priority in the profile, in rank order: ✅ good fit, ⚠️ mixed or unknown, ❌ poor fit, with one line of evidence each. Then weigh:
- **Experience overlap:** where {{USER}}'s background transfers directly, and where there's a learning curve to name honestly.
- **What they want vs. what {{USER}} has:** the role's must-haves against the profile's strengths and gaps.
- **Level:** is the role at the seniority {{USER}} wants, or a step back?
- **Stage and risk:** runway, profitability, layoffs, against {{USER}}'s appetite for risk.
- **Pay and work mode:** against the profile's floor and work-mode setting.
- **Trajectory fit:** does this role move {{USER}} toward the 3–5 year goal in the profile? How does it read on the CV?
- **Deal-breakers:** any hit goes at the very top of the cons.

Be candid. A con that kills the application belongs at the top, not buried.

**What they look for:** <2 lines: traits and experience they hire for>.

**Fit against your priorities**
| Priority | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| <#1> | ✅/⚠️/❌ | <evidence> |

**Pros**
- <specific to {{USER}}'s background, not generic praise>

**Cons / risks**
- <...>

Then offer in one line to save it. If {{USER}} says yes, write it to `{{DATA}}/Companies/<Company>.md` with the research date at the top, and offer to add the company to `{{WATCH}}`'s list if it isn't there yet (follow the Maintenance section of `{{WATCH}}`).

## Tips & Gotchas

- Funding and headcount go stale fast; always say when a figure is from.
- Glassdoor and LinkedIn often block fetching; use search snippets and say they're snippets.
- Don't write to the tracker from this skill. If {{USER}} says they'll apply, mention `{{TRACKER}}` can log it.
